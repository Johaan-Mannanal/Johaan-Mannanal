### VITAL at Purdue: Research

**AI Stroke Detection Researcher · September 2026 to present**

- Part of a five-person team designing a comparison of ADS and Rapid MRI for detecting and measuring acute ischemic stroke lesions on diffusion-weighted MRI, using expert annotations as the reference standard.
- The planned evaluation covers missed lesions, false positives, segmentation overlap, and lesion-volume error, with particular attention to small lesions. We will compare existing systems; no model training is currently planned.
- **Current stage: feasibility and study design.** Research access and dataset compatibility still need confirmation. We are investigating accessible, locally run tools, not claiming clinical equivalence or proven cost savings.

## Selected Projects

- **[Midnight](https://github.com/Johaan-Mannanal/midnight-product-case-study):** My product and engineering work on a course-grounded AI tutor, including the beta-to-rebuild transition, sanitized architecture, and clearly scoped metrics.
- **[Kamino](https://github.com/Johaan-Mannanal/kamino):** Co-built with a four-person team at Cal Hacks 13.0. The hosted workspace turns photos into reviewable 3D assets and portable MuJoCo bundles. A separate SO-101 prototype takes leader-arm recordings through simulation adjustment and export to physical follower-arm execution. Hardware execution is human-reviewed, and calibration remains ongoing; the hosted site does not control the arm. Public access needs no Kamino access code, with shared usage limits. New photo reconstructions require your own Meshy API key, an API-enabled Meshy plan, and Meshy credits; the synthetic demo needs no key. [Try Kamino](https://kamino.design) · [Robotics demo](https://github.com/Johaan-Mannanal/kamino/blob/main/docs/ROBOTICS_DEMO.md).
- **[OpenHouse Intelligence](https://github.com/tobywashere/open-house-intelligence-crm):** Local-first AI CRM. I built the dashboard and integrated eight approval-gated actions with the REST API. Our four-person team's initial one-day prototype placed Top 8 at the Dell × NVIDIA BuilderBase Hackathon in Seattle; development continued afterward.
- **[Wearable Health ML Research](https://github.com/Rhthm360/telemetry-healthcare):** Reproducible synthetic-data reconstruction of earlier wearable research. I built the ML pipeline and FastAPI backend; Yash Piratla built the iOS app. Benchmark results are not clinical evidence. [My case study](https://github.com/Johaan-Mannanal/wearable-health-case-study).
